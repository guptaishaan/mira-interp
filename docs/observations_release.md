The quality-qualified observational study completed on **53 matches, 424 clips,
13,568 rows and all 17 residual sites**, using Alakazam MIRA Mini 4P. The frozen
block-5 readout achieved 0.8469 standardized MSE on 11 confirmation matches,
20.1% below the discovery-mean baseline. Ball-position decoding was strongest;
several velocity coordinates remained weak.

These are teacher-forced annotation-decoding results with target pixels present.
The original 61-match data gate failed, and a separate 53-match quality-qualified
protocol was registered before research capture. Causal identification, geometry,
SAE/BSF recovery and steering remain unrun because their prerequisites are unmet.

The archive contains every pooled research feature and baseline, complete row
identities, all 17 full reserved-pilot residual tensors, completed audits and
attribution. Raw source videos and physical annotations (`y`) are excluded;
reproducing labels requires the pinned Rocket Science source and its access terms.
Every derived array was checked against the audited aggregate, and every finished
archive member was read back and hashed. Frozen probe coefficients, full metrics,
CSVs, PNG/PDF figures, code and documentation are committed in the repository.
