Complete generated artifacts for the reserved engineering pilot of MIRA Mini 4P steering.

This release contains both saved conditions: the baseline and the fixed quadratic-path +300 height edit. It includes every generated future frame and latent, the native/edited residual tiles and descriptors, frozen VideoMAE predictions, registrations, audits, exact experiment code, and model/source attribution. Lossless output arrays passed independent generation and measurement audits and exact parity with the original storage format. These checks establish reproducible intervention wiring, not successful physical steering.

Download the numbered tar parts, their JSON sidecars, `archive_manifest.json`, and the completed package report. Each tar is standalone; its `PART_MANIFEST.json` lists exact members and hashes. Future video has shape [4,8,3,288,512] and future latents [4,4,9,16,32]. Source Rocket Science context, simulator labels, actions, and pretrained model checkpoints are excluded. Reproducing predictor forward passes requires the authorized observed context.

The full selection/confirmation study is separate from this reserved pilot. Pilot videos and comparison figures are also available in the repository under `results/rollout_steering_v1/pilot_previews/`. The executable model is the independent Alakazam MIRA Mini 4P reproduction, not the original 5B MIRA.
