Complete selection artifacts for the registered MIRA Mini 4P rollout experiment: 10 matches, two paired seeds, and all 21 conditions per pair (420 outputs).

The five fixed paths are probe linear, affine map, quadratic map, norm-matched random, and wrong-variable ball x. Requested doses are -600, -300, 0, +300, and +600; the zero baseline is shared. Effective doses and clipping are recorded. There is one residual edit at the first generated latent, with actions fixed and later latent steps unedited.

Every condition is included. Numbered tar parts contain future-only frame/latent tensors, native and edited activations, complete frozen VideoMAE predictions, metadata, registrations, independent generation/measurement audits, source code, and attribution. Each part has an embedded manifest and a JSON sidecar; `archive_manifest.json` describes the complete package. Use their SHA256 values to verify downloads.

Generated measurements are learned video estimates. Their accuracy on edited generated clips is unverified. Publication and successful execution do not establish reliable physical control. This exploratory study remains separate from the initial held-out decoding results shown in the README.

Observed Rocket Science context, action arrays, simulator-label arrays, and pretrained checkpoints are excluded. The executable model is Alakazam's independent MIRA Mini 4P reproduction, not the original 5B MIRA.
